Tell Me About Newsletters

I'm researching the newsletter space — as products, as business models, and as reader experiences. Four quick questions, no wrong answers. Takes two minutes.

Four Questions

  1. How many newsletters do you subscribe to? (0–3, 4–6, 7+)
  2. Which newsletters really break through for you, and why?
  3. If you were taking over a newsletter portfolio today, the first thing you'd do is ______.
  4. One important thing everyone gets wrong about newsletters is ______.
