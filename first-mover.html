Content Strategy

How we turned a boring spreadsheet into a compelling story about new business growth

Video Production · Multi-Channel Campaign

My Role

Scoped, storyboarded, wrote the script for, directed, and edited the video. Wrote, edited, and vetted the blog post. Created the social post copy, along with a copy package for the webinar, promotional emails, newsletters, and additional social posts.

The Challenge

Data about pipeline growth and first-mover advantage in sales existed as dry spreadsheets. The challenge was transforming complex sales intelligence data into a narrative that would resonate with sales leaders and drive engagement across multiple channels.

The Solution

Created an integrated content campaign that brought the data to life through storytelling. The centerpiece video used clear visualizations and a compelling narrative to demonstrate how sales teams can gain competitive advantage by being first to new opportunities.

The campaign extended beyond video to include a comprehensive blog post, social media strategy, webinar materials, and email marketing—ensuring consistent messaging across all customer touchpoints.

The Impact

The multi-channel approach transformed technical product data into an engaging story about sales success, making complex pipeline analytics accessible and actionable for the target audience.
